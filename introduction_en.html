Questions You Might Have

Do I need an internet connection?

No. All AI models and image processing run entirely offline on your device. After installation from the Microsoft Store, you never need an internet connection — your photos never leave your computer.

What image formats are supported?

Import: PNG, JPEG, WebP, BMP, TIFF. Export: PNG, JPEG, WebP, BMP, TIFF — all with adjustable quality settings where applicable.

Is my original photo overwritten?

Never. PhotoRestore Pro always saves results as new files. Your original images are never modified — they serve as your permanent backup.

Is there a free trial? How does it work?

Yes. Download the free trial from the Microsoft Store — no credit card, no account required. The trial gives you full access to all features with limited AI processing per session. Purchase a license anytime for unlimited AI processing and batch operations.

What's the difference between AI and Fast restoration?

AI Restoration uses a deep-learning pipeline (15–60s) for severely aged photos. Fast Restoration uses traditional algorithms (under 1s) for lightly faded photos — choose based on your photo's condition.

Can I use it for commercial work?

Yes. Photos processed with PhotoRestore Pro can be used for commercial purposes — client work, prints, publishing, social media. No usage restrictions or watermarks on exported images.

Is my data collected or shared?

No data collection of any kind. PhotoRestore Pro does not include analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, or any network communication. Every pixel is processed exclusively in your computer's memory.

All purchases are processed through the Microsoft Store, which provides a standard refund policy. Try the free trial first to ensure the app meets your needs before purchasing.
