## Canonical policy

The introduction pages for the ten languages published on haoyong123ai.com
(en, ja, ko, de, fr, es, pt, ru, zh, ar) declare a cross-domain canonical
pointing at the official product page. The remaining thirteen languages
(it, hi, nl, pl, ro, sv, th, tl, tr, uk, vi, id, bn) are self-canonical and are
the primary version for those markets. Help pages declare a self-canonical URL;
privacy pages do not declare a canonical and therefore resolve to themselves.
